Delivery & returns

Orders are prepared and handed over by the shop owner. There is no courier company involved.

How you can get your order

OptionFeeHow it works
UCC campus pickupFreeCollect in person at the shop's pickup point on UCC campus.
Junction collectionGHS 5.00Your order is sent by a public transport driver to the junction you name. You collect it there.
House deliveryGHS 10.00Same as junction collection, but carried on from the junction to your address.

Pickup code

After you place an order you get a six-digit pickup code. Show it when you collect. It works once.

Payment

Payment arrangements are confirmed with you after your order is placed.

Returns

Check your perfume when you collect it. If it is damaged or not what you ordered, contact us the same day and we will sort it out. Perfume that has been opened or used cannot be returned.
