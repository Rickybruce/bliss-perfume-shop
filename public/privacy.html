Privacy

What this shop stores about you and why.

What we collect

  • Username, email address and phone number, to create your account and verify your phone.
  • Your order history, and the junction or delivery address you give us, so we can hand over your order.

How it is used

Only to run your orders and contact you about them. We do not sell your details or share them for advertising.

Passwords and sign-in

Passwords are stored hashed, never as plain text. Signing in uses a secure session cookie. Your cart is kept in your own browser until you check out.

Your details

To correct or delete your account, contact the shop.
